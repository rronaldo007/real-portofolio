Case-study body

section 03 / 06

The brief. A self-hosted project management tool for distributed teams who don't live in their inbox. Most of the team had tried Asana, Notion, Linear — and all of them felt either too heavy or too prescribed.

Mosaic started as a weekend experiment with HTMX on top of Django. The idea was simple: render full pages on the server, swap fragments in place, never write a single line of JavaScript framework code. Three months later it was running ourselves and four other studios.
